The core visual mechanism is not ordinary overlapping but successive layers of same-colour translucent graphics. Build the subject from several related-colour, semitransparent, mutually interpenetrating silhouettes, like transparent spot-colour ink repeatedly printed on paper. Spatial relationships are about passing through and overprinting, not front-to-back occlusion. Control overlap area, shape direction and ink density to produce rhythmic tonal variation, avoiding uniform layering.
